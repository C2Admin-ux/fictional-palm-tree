# Sunday Reset — brain dump + week setup

One page, ~40 minutes. Goal: empty head, app that matches reality, a Monday you believe.

## 1. Brain dump — 10 min

Tasks page, press **n** (or **Cmd+K** from anywhere, or the **+** in the mobile top bar). Type everything on your mind, one entry at a time — the parser does the filing as you type:

> `call plumber fox hill tomorrow !urgent` · `gather tax docs by fri` · `#rock refi package outline`

Property names, dates (`tomorrow`, `fri`, `sep 15`, `by friday`), priority (`!urgent` / `!high` / `!low`), and `#tags` are all parsed and stripped from the title. **Don't organize while dumping.** No date, no property is fine — it lands in your Inbox for step 2.

## 2. Inbox to zero — 5 min

Tasks → **Review**. Top section is your Inbox. For each item, one of four moves:

- **Do it** now if it's under 2 minutes
- **Date it** — `d` for the due menu
- **Snooze it** — `s` — if it's real but not this week
- **Delete it** if it stopped mattering

Nothing stays in Inbox.

## 3. Sweep the rest of Review — 10 min

- **Waiting on** — aging items on PMs/vendors. Anything stale: task yourself a nudge for Monday, or snooze to the honest follow-up date.
- **Obligations horizon** — auto deadlines (insurance, contracts, renewals) in the next 90 days. Anything that needs motion this week gets a due date now.
- **Rocks** — the quarter's big things. Each rock should have a concrete next step due this week; if a rock hasn't moved in two Sundays, decide why. (Mountain icon on any task row toggles rock status.)
- **Shipped last 7 days** — momentum check; also your material for PM calls and investor updates.

## 4. Freshen the data — 10 min

The app is only useful Monday–Friday if Sunday makes it match reality:

- **Renewals** (`/renewals`) — log any offer/approval emails from the week (received + approved legs). The snoozed review task *is* the chase — snooze it to when you expect the PM to deliver. Enter the renewal rate if a month just closed.
- **CapEx** — move projects whose status changed; jot bid notes from the week's vendor calls; act on anything in the dashboard's **Decisions waiting** lane (bids ready to compare, cancel windows, renewals).
- **Findings** — triage anything captured on walks this week: watch / flag / spawn task / capex / resolved. Born-untriaged findings help no one.
- **Litigation** — deadlines inside 14 days show red on the board. Make sure each has a task or calendar block.
- **Calls** — confirm or dismiss the extracted items from this week's PM call notes so they become real tasks (or disappear).

## 5. Shape the week — 5 min

Tasks → **Agenda**. Drive the **Overdue** group to zero — complete it, postpone it honestly (`p` = +1 day, `d` → +1 week), or snooze it past this week. Use `x` to multi-select, then batch due/snooze/priority from the bar. Finish by looking at the dashboard **Today** lane as Monday will see it: if it isn't doable in one day, push more now — Sunday-you is the only one who'll admit it.

**Keys:** `j`/`k` move · `n` quick-add · `c` complete · `e` edit · `s` snooze · `d` due · `p` postpone +1d · `x` select · `l`/`h` subtasks · `Cmd+K` palette anywhere
